# Vivenda — live demo

A community shopping app that connects local businesses with young people through products, stories and events — **one app for both customers and shop owners**.
Live demo of my UX/UI case study, built as a mobile web app (PWA) from the original Figma design.

**Live:** https://vivenda.sezaigocmen.com · **Case study:** https://www.sezaigocmen.com/projects/vivenda

## What you can try

- **Discover** — your neighborhood at a glance: shop story rings, shops near you, this week’s workshops, new products
- **Categories → shop → product** — six local shops, each with its own page, sub-categories and products
- **Stories** — meet the people behind each shop in full-screen, Instagram-style photo stories (swipe between shops)
- **Favorites, cart and checkout** — bike delivery or pickup, demo payment, order confirmation and your local impact
- **Workshops** — book a spot at a sourdough, pottery or knitting class
- **Customer / Business switch** — in Profile, switch to the Cozy Bakery owner’s dashboard: sales, incoming orders (including yours) and bookings

On a phone it runs full-screen like a native app (Share → Add to Home Screen). On desktop it shows an iPhone mockup with a QR code.

## Demo notes

- No backend: sample data and everything you do are stored only in your browser (localStorage).
- No real accounts, payments or deliveries.
